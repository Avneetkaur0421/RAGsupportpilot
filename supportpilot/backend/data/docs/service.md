# Service and repairs

## Tune-ups
We recommend a tune-up every 1,000 miles or every 6 months, whichever comes first.

## Where to get service
Any local bike shop can service standard parts. Motor and battery repairs must be done by a Kestrel-certified shop to keep the warranty valid.

## Spare parts
Spare batteries ($549), chargers ($59) and replacement brake pads ($18) are available in the accessories store.

## Error codes
If the display shows an error code, turn the bike off for 30 seconds and back on. If the code comes back, note it and contact support.
