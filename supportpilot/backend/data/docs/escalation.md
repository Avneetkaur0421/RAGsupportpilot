# Escalation and tone guide

## When to escalate
Escalate to a team lead for safety issues (brake failure, battery swelling, smoke or overheating), legal threats, press enquiries, or any refund above your approval limit.

## Battery safety
If a customer reports a swollen, smoking or very hot battery, tell them to stop using and charging it immediately and move it away from anything flammable. Escalate it as urgent the same day.

## Response times
First responses are due within 4 business hours for email and 2 minutes for live chat. Urgent safety cases get a callback within 1 hour.

## Tone
Use the customer's name, acknowledge the problem in one sentence, then give the next step. Don't blame the customer or the carrier.
