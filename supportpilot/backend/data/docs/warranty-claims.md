# Warranty claim processing

## Checking a claim
Confirm the purchase date in the order system, and check the photos or video for crash damage or modifications before approving.

## Replacing parts
Approved claims for batteries, motors and displays get a free replacement part, plus a prepaid label to return the faulty one within 14 days.

## Frame claims
Frame claims always need a team lead's review and a photo of the serial number, which is under the bottom bracket.
