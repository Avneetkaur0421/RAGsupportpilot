# Warranty

## Coverage
Frames are covered for 5 years. Motors, batteries and electronics are covered for 2 years. Other components are covered for 1 year.

## What isn't covered
Wear items such as tires, brake pads, chains and grips aren't covered. Damage from crashes, submerging the bike in water, or third-party modifications isn't covered.

## Battery capacity
A battery is covered if it drops below 70% of its original capacity within 2 years of normal use.

## Making a claim
To make a warranty claim, email support with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos or a short video showing the problem.
