# Battery care and charging

## Charging time
A full charge from empty takes about 5 hours with the included 3A charger. The optional fast charger ($129) cuts this to about 3 hours.

## Range
Range is 40 to 70 miles per charge, depending on rider weight, terrain, pedal-assist level and temperature.

## Storage
For storage longer than a month, keep the battery at 40 to 60% charge in a dry place between 50°F and 77°F, and top it up every 2 months.

## Cold weather
Charge the battery at room temperature. Riding below 32°F can reduce range by up to 30%. This is temporary and not a defect.

## Rain and water
The battery and motor are rated IPX5, so riding in rain is fine. Don't submerge the bike or clean it with a pressure washer.
