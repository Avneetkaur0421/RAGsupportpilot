# Assembly and first ride

## What's in the box
Bikes arrive about 85% assembled. You attach the handlebar, front wheel, pedals and front light. Tools are included.

## Assembly time
Most riders finish assembly in 30 to 45 minutes by following the video guide on our website.

## First ride check
Before your first ride, check tire pressure (50 to 65 PSI), test both brakes, and fully charge the battery.

## Professional assembly
If you'd rather not assemble it yourself, add professional assembly at checkout for $89, done by our partner bike shops.
