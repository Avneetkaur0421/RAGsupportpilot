# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Orders ship within 2 business days from our warehouse. Standard delivery takes 4 to 7 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days and costs $39.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on all bikes. Accessory orders under $50 ship for $6.95, and accessory orders of $50 or more ship free.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. We can't ship to PO boxes because bikes need a signature on delivery. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed when your order leaves the warehouse. If tracking hasn't updated for 3 business days, contact support with your order number.
