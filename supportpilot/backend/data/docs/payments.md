# Payments and financing

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.

## Financing
Financing is available through our lending partner for orders over $500, with 6, 12 or 24 month plans. Approval and rates depend on the partner's credit check.

## Price drops
If we lower the price of your bike within 14 days of purchase, contact support and we'll refund the difference.
